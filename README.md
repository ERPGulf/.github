# ERPGulf

Frappe and ERPNext specialists serving businesses across the Gulf region,
Malaysia and Singapore.

We build award-winning apps and implement ERPNext solutions with a focus
on regional compliance, point of sale, mobile, HR and AI.

## What We Do

- **ERPNext Implementation** – end-to-end Frappe/ERPNext deployments,
  customization and support
- **ZATCA E-Invoicing** – Saudi Arabia Phase 2 compliant e-invoicing
  for ERPNext
- **UAE E-Invoicing** – FTA / Peppol (PINT-AE) integration for ERPNext
- **Malaysia E-Invoicing** – LHDN MyInvois integration for ERPNext
- **GPos** – point of sale for ERPNext with ZATCA compliance and MADA
  payment terminal integration. Winner of the Best App Award 2026
- **changAI** – AI for ERPNext
- **Mobile Apps** – mobile apps connected to ERPNext for sales, POS,
  employees and field teams
- **HRMS & Employee Self-Service** – HR, payroll and mobile employee apps
- **Cloud Infrastructure** – hosting and managed services on OCI and Azure

## Industries

- **Retail & POS** – multi-store retail, point of sale and payment

- **Real Estate** – property, leasing and tenant management
- **Manufacturing** – production, inventory and costing
